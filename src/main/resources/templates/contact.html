Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping takes 2-3 business days. You'll receive tracking details via email.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging. Free returns on all orders.
Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, we ship worldwide! International delivery takes 7-14 business days. Shipping costs calculated at checkout.
How can I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track it in your account under "My Orders".
